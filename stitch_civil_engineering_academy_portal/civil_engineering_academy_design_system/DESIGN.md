## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authoritative, prestigious, and rigorous architectural character tailored for high-stakes public service engineering preparation (GPSC, GSSSB, GPSSB). The aesthetic harmonizes classical engineering governance with contemporary edtech refinement.

### Design Movement & Mood
- **Style Blend:** Modern Institutional with Tactile Blueprint Accents. Structural alignment, crisp golden rule-lines, and deep architectural blue volumes dominate the layout.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, disciplined, encouraging, and prestigious.
- **Audience:** Aspiring civil engineers, diploma holders, and graduate officers demanding absolute clarity, reliability, and academic excellence.

## Colors

The palette references classical drafting rooms, governmental prestige, and gold-standard credentials.

- **Primary (`#2B3A55` - Twilight Indigo):** Conveys stability, deep academic rigor, and governmental authority. Used for major headers, primary actions, dense navigational chrome, and structural headers.
- **Secondary / Neutral Slate (`#566277` - Blue Slate):** Technical neutral used for supporting labels, drafting metadata, subheaders, inactive tabs, and secondary buttons.
- **Brand Highlight Accent (`#D4AB3F` - Metallic Gold):** High-prestige indicator used for merit badges, callouts, rank badges, primary CTAs needing conversion contrast, and progress trackers.
- **Border & Structural Detail (`#B89E58` - Golden Bronze):** Used for architectural hairline borders, section dividers, card contours, and technical framing lines.
- **Warm Neutral Foundation (`#F6F0DF` - Ivory Mist):** The default canvas tone. Prevents the glare of pure digital white and imparts an archival parchment feel suitable for intensive study.
- **Pure Canvas Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for card surfaces, test consoles, and reading tiles to guarantee optimal text contrast against the ivory canvas backdrop.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, relying on weight variation (800 down to 400) and letter-spacing shifts to distinguish structural blueprints from narrative explanations.

- **Headlines & Display:** Tightly tracked with negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a solid, stamped typographical presence reminiscent of civil engineering blueprints.
- **Labels & Badges:** Slightly tracked out (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) in all-caps or semibold weights to provide quick scanability in dense exam series listings, syllabus matrices, and paper breakdowns.
- **Body Copy:** Set at standard leading for effortless reading of lengthy syllabus outlines, technical question explanations, and administrative exam circulars.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system anchors the web application, adapting to 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` side margins.
  - Tablet (768px – 1024px): 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` side margins.
  - Mobile (<768px): 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` side margins.
- **Cadence & Rhythm:** Components snap strictly to multiples of 4px and 8px. Spacing between major engineering curriculum modules relies on `space-xl`, while inline metadata (question counters, marks allotted, negative marking tags) uses `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an academic, document-centric aesthetic, elevation favors **low-contrast architectural outlines and subtle ambient depth** over heavy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Base):** Ivory Mist canvas (`#F6F0DF`) for the overall page structure.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Tier):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) containers framed by a `1px` crisp border in Golden Bronze with reduced opacity (`#B89E58` at 30% alpha).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Hovered Modules):** Pure White containers with `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 58, 85, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 58, 85, 0.04)` and a refined `#B89E58` border.
- **Level 3 (Modal Test Screens, Drawer Navs):** Framed in `#2B3A55` hairline edge with `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(43, 58, 85, 0.16)`.
- **Nav & Footer Shell:** Twilight Indigo (`#2B3A55`) surfaces anchored by a continuous bottom/top border of `2px solid #D4AB3F` (Metallic Gold).

## Shapes

The academy’s shape language embraces **Level 1 (Soft)** geometry. Civil engineering demands precision, stability, and structure; hyper-rounded or pill-shaped designs dilute the discipline’s institutional weight.

- **Base Radius:** `0.25rem` (4px) for badges, checkboxes, inputs, and tabular rows.
- **Module & Card Radius (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px) for syllabus cards, test paper blocks, and lecture drawers.
- **Structural Overlays (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px) maximum for high-tier interactive modals and admission alerts.
- **Tags & Status Indicators:** Retain strict 4px corners to replicate technical drafting labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#2B3A55`, text in `#FFFFFF`, with a subtle inset bottom border (`2px solid #1C2638`). On hover, background shifts to `#36496B`.
- **Accent (Call-to-Action / Enroll):** Background in `#D4AB3F`, text in `#2B3A55` (Bold), with a `1px solid #B89E58` perimeter. On hover, background lightens to `#DFB952`.
- **Secondary / Technical:** Outlined in `1.5px solid #566277`, text in `#2B3A55`, transparent fill. Hover yields a faint wash of `#566277` at 8% opacity.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background is `#FFFFFF` against the `#F6F0DF` canvas.
- Border is `1px solid #B89E58` (50% opacity), rounding at `0.25rem`.
- Focus state activates a `1.5px solid #2B3A55` border accompanied by an ambient halo of `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 171, 63, 0.25)`.
- Placeholder text is set in `#566277` with regular weight.

### Cards (Courseware, Exam Series & Faculty)
- Background `#FFFFFF` sitting on the `#F6F0DF` canvas.
- Perimeter defined by a crisp `1px solid rgba(184, 158, 88, 0.35)`.
- Card headers feature an optional architectural accent: a top border strip of `3px solid #2B3A55` or `#D4AB3F` based on track urgency (e.g., GPSC Class 1-2 vs. Assistant Engineer).

### Chips & Badges
- **Exam Category Chip (e.g., "GPSC Executive Engineer"):** Solid `#2B3A55` pill or soft tag with `#F6F0DF` lettering.
- **Status / Merit Badge (e.g., "Rank 1", "Verified Syllabus"):** `#F6F0DF` background, `#B89E58` border (`1px`), text in `#2B3A55` with bold tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with `2px` radius for checkboxes; circular for test option radios.
- Inactive: `1.5px solid #566277`.
- Selected: Solid `#2B3A55` fill with pure white tick or central radio dot, outlined with a fine `#D4AB3F` ring when active.

### Academic Test Console & Timers
- Dedicated assessment cards feature high-contrast Twilight Indigo status bars with Metallic Gold countdown glyphs.
- Monospace or high-legibility numerals styled with `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold figures ensure instant readability during simulated mock tests.
