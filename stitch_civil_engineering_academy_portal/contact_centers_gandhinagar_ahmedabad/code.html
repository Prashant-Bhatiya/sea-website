Frequently Asked Inquiries

Visiting & Admissions FAQ

help Can I attend a free offline demo lecture before enrolling?

Yes. Candidates may attend up to two consecutive technical subject lectures (SOM, Surveying, or RCC) at either the Gandhinagar or Ahmedabad center with prior desk token registration.

help Is parking available for aspirants at Gandhinagar HQ?

Ample two-wheeler and four-wheeler designated parking is available within the Sardar Patel Bhavan complex perimeter with CCTV security surveillance.

help What are the library timings for registered cadre students?

The Gandhinagar Technical Library operates 24/7 with biometric access. Non-enrolled students may also access reading facilities through daily pass permits upon vacancy.

help Can I transfer between Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar batches?

Yes, syllabus schedules across both centers run in synchronization. Aspirants can seamlessly request campus rotation through their student portal credentials.
